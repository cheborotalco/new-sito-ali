AI Innovations · Smart Devices

AI Hospitality: Smart Noise-Canceling Beds

Project Objective

To create and bring to the French hospitality market an innovative B2B solution, namely next-generation smart beds with integrated AI algorithms and active noise cancellation (ANC) acoustic systems to enhance guest privacy and rest quality.

Expert Role

End-to-end coordination of product development and launch: synchronizing acoustic engineers, furniture technologists, and AI developers, overseeing hardware and software integration, and adapting the solution to hospitality industry standards.

Decisions Made

Implementation of adaptive AI algorithms that analyze external background noise in the room in real time and generate anti-phase acoustic waves, creating an individual "quiet zone" around the sleeping area, without the need for additional structural wall soundproofing.

Final Result

Successful launch of the product for the hospitality segment in France, creation of a new competitive advantage for hotels, increasing guest satisfaction scores and average check, as well as validation of a complex hardware+AI solution in a real commercial environment.
